🔥 Comprehensive Feature Tour

1. Point of Sale & Register Control (Retail)

  • High-Speed Cashier UI: Fast category navigation, product search, touch grid, and quick discount keys.
  • Flexible Barcode Scanning: Works natively with handheld USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners (HID), built-in camera scanning, or manual entry.
  • Weighing-Scale Barcodes: Automatically decodes price/weight barcodes generated by electronic butcher and deli scales.
  • Split Payments & Held Orders: Accept payment split across Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, and Credit. Park unfinished orders and resume anytime.
  • Shift Management & Z-Reports: Cash drawer opening float, petty cash in/out tracking, and end-of-day discrepancy reporting.
  • ESC/POS Thermal Printing: Direct 80mm & 58mm receipt printing over Bluetooth, Network LAN/Wi-Fi, and USB with auto cash-drawer kick.

2. Restaurant, Café & Quick-Service Module

  • Visual Floor & Table Layout: Real-time color-coded table occupancy (Free, Occupied, Reserved, Needs Cleaning).
  • Kitchen Display System (KDS): Interactive kitchen terminal showing live order cards, preparation stages, and elapsed-time alerts.
  • Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT): Route orders directly to kitchen thermal printers separated by food/beverage preparation stations.
  • Dine-In, Takeaway & Delivery: Seamless workflow for table seating, phone orders, and takeout counters.
  • QR Code Contactless Ordering: Place table stand cards with QR codes so guests can browse digital menus and order from their phones.

3. Specialized Industry Add-On Verticals

  • 💊 Pharmacy & Healthcare: Batch/Lot number tracking on receipt & sale, expiry date warning flags, doctor prescription attachment, and controlled dispensing logs.
  • ✂️ Salon & Spa Management: Visual appointment booking calendar, specialist/stylist chair allocation, service durations, and staff commission & tip reports.
  • 🔧 Device Repair Workbench: Device intake tickets with condition checklist, diagnostics findings, spare parts & labor billing, and public customer tracking link.
  • 📊 Lead Management & CRM: Visual Kanban deal pipeline, lead source tracking, follow-up scheduling with reminders, and 1-click won deal to customer invoice conversion.

4. Multi-Store Warehousing & Inventory

  • Multi-Branch Architecture: Centralized master product catalog with branch-specific stock levels, pricing, and cashier permissions.
  • Inter-Branch Stock Transfers: Move inventory between outlets and warehouses with dispatch & receiving confirmation.
  • Bulk Import & Export: Upload thousands of products, variants, SKUs, and stock quantities via Excel/CSV spreadsheets.
  • AI Product Image Generator: Built-in AI imagery engine to automatically generate photo-ready product catalog visuals.
  • Low Stock & Expiry Alarms: Automated alerts notify store managers before inventory runs out.

5. Customer Ledger, Credit & Finance

  • Accounts Receivable (Customer Credit): Issue credit sales with custom limits, track outstanding balances, and record partial settlements.
  • Automated Payment Reminders: Send scheduled WhatsApp, SMS, and Email payment reminders to customers with overdue balances.
  • Accounts Payable (Supplier Bills): Manage purchase orders, vendor invoices, and track payment schedules.
  • Customer Loyalty Points: Reward repeat shoppers with points earned on purchases redeemable against future orders.
  • Tax Engine & Fiscal Compliance: Multi-rate tax rules, inclusive/exclusive calculations, and QR fiscal receipt support.

6. Built-in E-Commerce Online Storefront

  • Instant Web Storefront: Every tenant can turn on a responsive e-commerce web store with shopping cart and wishlist.
  • Custom Domain Branding: Connect shop.clientdomain.com directly to their storefront.
  • Order Tracking Portal: Customers receive a live order tracking URL to follow fulfillment status from pending to delivered.
  • Discount Coupons & Promos: Percentage and fixed cart discounts with expiration dates and usage limits.
  • Customer Reviews & Moderation: Social proof review engine with admin approval controls.
