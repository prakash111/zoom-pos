🚀 How to Install Any Module (1-Minute Setup)

1
Open SuperAdmin
Sign in to your SuperAdmin portal at https://yourdomain.com/login and navigate to Modules.
2
Upload Module ZIP
Click Upload Module and select the downloaded .zip file (e.g. leadmanagement.zip).
3
Activate & Assign
Click Activate. For extensions, enable it under Tenants → Tenant Detail → Optional Extensions.
